SOUTH INDIAN · BANANA LEAF INVITATION — HOW TO EDIT
====================================================
Edit ONLY details.js (on GitHub: open the file > pencil > edit > Commit changes). Keep the "quotes" and commas.

Couple ....... groom / bride (short), groomFull / brideFull (invitation card), monogram, parents
Opening ...... gateEyebrow, gateText, gateImage (resort photo behind the envelope)
Pictures ..... heroImage (top), venueImage, footerImage, postcards (3 photos)
Date ......... weddingDateTime (countdown), dateDay / dateMonth / dateYear (3 scratch cards — shown on EVERY visit)
Events ....... the two function cards: title, day, date, month, time, extras (extra lines), note, image, mood ("evening"/"dawn")
Venue ........ venueName, venueAddress, venueNote, mapLink (paste a Google Maps share link) or mapQuery
Closing ...... closing, families, hashtag
Music ........ put an MP3 in the music folder; when replacing, use a NEW name (song2.mp3) and update music:
Hidden ....... hideSections: ["gallery","dress","rsvp"] — remove a name to show that section again
Photos ....... upload photos from INSIDE the images folder; use lowercase names like resort-lake.jpg
